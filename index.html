Three modes, one companion

    Live pick and ban help through the full 5v5 draft. Suggestions respect phase, bans, and who's already on the board. And every suggestion is filtered by what's actually in your box.

    Build 3v3 defenses from your roster that other players will struggle to beat. Or face a known enemy defense and get counter teams ranked for your exact box. Track your personal wins and losses across every defense you've faced.
